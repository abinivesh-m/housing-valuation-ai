Every plan returns the same engine

Plans differ in volume and support, never in model quality. A 50-call trial sees exactly what a lender sees.

Calibrated, not asserted

Split-conformal intervals measured at 79.1% empirical coverage against a nominal 80%. The band widens for unseen micro-markets instead of hiding the uncertainty.

Attribution that reconciles

Baseline-substitution effects in rupees, batched into one prediction pass. Effects plus the reported residual equal the valuation.

An independent cross-check

Comparable sales with an adjustment grid built separately from the model, so agreement between the two is evidence rather than a tautology.

A policy position

RBI/NHB LTV bands, a confidence haircut that only ever tightens, six named checks, eight reason codes, and a maximum loan sized on the p10.

A tamper-evident trail

Every valuation is hash-chained. The verify endpoint recomputes the chain and names the first broken link.

No data account required

The engine carries its own model and artifacts. You do not need a property data subscription, a Zillow key, or a scraping pipeline.

Questions buyers actually ask

What is the training data?

A documented parametric corpus, not licensed transaction data — no redistributable national transaction dataset exists in India. Within-corpus MAPE is 11.16% against 12.86% for a locality rate card, and the repository says so in the first screen. The engine is a working system ready for a registry feed, not a claim about market accuracy. If you need production accuracy today, this is not the product for you, and we would rather say that before you pay.

Can I use the output in a credit decision?

No. The engine evaluates a collateral-risk policy and returns a position with reason codes. It does not see the borrower and does not make a credit decision. A lender remains the decision-maker and is responsible for validation and fair-lending review.

How does billing work?

Monthly, in advance, by card. Keys are issued per workspace and quota resets on the calendar month. Usage is visible at /api/v1/usage at any time. Cancel before renewal and the key stops at the end of the paid period.

What happens if I exceed my plan?

Calls continue and are billed at your plan's overage rate. The Explore plan is the exception: it stops at 50 calls so a trial cannot generate an unexpected invoice.

Do you offer refunds?

If the engine returns an error or a malformed response, the call is not billed and the month is credited. We do not refund a month of successful calls because the valuations were inconvenient.

Is there an SLA?

Team and Lender plans carry a 99.5% monthly availability target on the valuation endpoint, excluding scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours ahead. Explore and Developer are best-effort.

Read this before buying. The model is trained on a synthetic corpus and every accuracy figure is within-corpus. The interval, attribution, comparable grid, policy engine and audit chain are real and tested. The market accuracy is not proven. Buy this as a working valuation system you can point at your own data, not as a certified valuer.
