Terms of use

Last updated 2026-11-25

1. What this is

The engine is an automated valuation model. It produces a model estimate of property value, a calibrated interval, rupee attribution, comparable sales and a collateral-risk policy position. It is not a certified valuation, not a registered valuer's report, and not a credit decision. It does not see the borrower, the title, the encumbrance or the fraud risk, and it must not be the sole basis for a lending decision.

2. Data and accuracy

The shipped model is trained on a documented parametric (synthetic) corpus. Every accuracy figure in the repository and on the site is within-corpus. Production accuracy can only be established against the buyer's own transaction data, which the buyer supplies under their own licence. We make no claim about market accuracy and we will not be held to one.
